Serene Backwaters of Kerala

Explore Kerala’s tranquil backwaters, a network of canals, lagoons, and lakes surrounded by lush greenery.

Houseboat Experience in Alleppey

Alleppey, known as the "Venice of the East," offers enchanting houseboat cruises through its calm backwaters. Enjoy luxurious accommodations, traditional Kerala meals, and mesmerizing views of swaying palms, paddy fields, and serene waters.

Kumarakom Backwaters

Kumarakom is a picturesque village situated on the banks of Vembanad Lake. Known for its bird sanctuary and peaceful backwater cruises, it is an ideal destination for birdwatchers and nature lovers. The scenic beauty and tranquil ambiance make it a must-visit spot.

Ashtamudi Lake

Ashtamudi Lake, located in Kollam, is known for its eight arms or channels that stretch across the region. A popular backwater destination, it offers a serene retreat for visitors seeking a quieter and less crowded experience compared to Alleppey and Kumarakom.

Poovar Island

Poovar Island offers a unique blend of beach and backwater experiences. This serene destination near Trivandrum features golden sand beaches, tranquil backwaters, and floating cottages, making it a perfect getaway for relaxation and photography.
