DISTRICTS OF KERALA

Kozhikode

Overview:

  • Location: Kozhikode, also known as Calicut, is a district located in the northern part of Kerala. Renowned for its historical significance, beautiful beaches, and vibrant culture, Kozhikode has a rich heritage that reflects its historical role as a major trading center.
  • Historical Significance:
    • Vasco Da Gama Square: The place where Vasco da Gama, the famous Portuguese explorer, landed in 1498. The square is a significant historical landmark and a popular tourist attraction.
    • Kozhikode Beach: An iconic beach with a historic lighthouse, offering picturesque views and a glimpse into the region's maritime history.
    • Pazhassi Raja Tomb: Dedicated to the "Lion of Kerala," Pazhassi Raja, a prominent figure in the resistance against British colonial rule.
  • Tourist Attractions:
    • Kappad Beach: Famous for being the landing site of Vasco da Gama, this serene beach is ideal for relaxation and offers beautiful sunset views.
    • Beypore: Known for its traditional shipbuilding industry, Beypore Beach is a popular spot for its calm waters and the historic Beypore Port.
    • Thusharagiri Waterfalls: A picturesque waterfall located in the Western Ghats, surrounded by lush green forests, ideal for nature lovers and trekkers.
  • Natural Beauty:
    • Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary: Partially extending into Kozhikode, this sanctuary is known for its rich biodiversity, including elephants, tigers, and a variety of bird species.
    • Peruvannamuzhi Dam: A serene reservoir surrounded by hills and lush greenery, offering boat rides and a tranquil environment.
  • Economy:
    • Trading and Commerce: Historically, Kozhikode was a major trading center, and today it remains an important commercial hub with a strong focus on trade and industry.
    • Handloom and Textiles: The district is known for its traditional handloom products, including the famous Kozhikode cloth and textiles.
  • Cultural Significance:
    • Cuisine: Kozhikode is renowned for its distinctive cuisine, including dishes like biryani, halwa, and various seafood delicacies.
    • Festivals: The district celebrates various festivals with fervor, including the Kozhikode Carnival, a vibrant event marking the New Year with music, dance, and cultural performances.
  • Educational Institutions:
    • Kozhikode Medical College: One of the oldest medical colleges in India, known for its contributions to medical education and healthcare.
    • NIT Calicut: The National Institute of Technology Calicut, a premier engineering institution offering high-quality education and research opportunities.
  • Transport and Connectivity:
    • Road and Rail: Kozhikode is well-connected by road and rail, with Kozhikode Railway Station serving as a major junction in the region.
    • Nearest Airport: The district is accessible from Calicut International Airport, located approximately 25 kilometers away, offering both domestic and international flights.
  • Tourism and Economy: Kozhikode is a district that embodies a unique blend of historical heritage, natural beauty, and vibrant culture, making it a fascinating destination for visitors and residents alike.

Kozhikode is a captivating district with a combination of cultural richness, historical landmarks, and natural beauty that makes it an exceptional part of Kerala.
