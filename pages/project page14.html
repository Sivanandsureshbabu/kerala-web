DISTRICTS OF KERALA

Kannur

Overview:

  • Location: Kannur is a district located in the northern part of Kerala. Known for its rich cultural heritage, historical significance, and scenic beauty, Kannur is often referred to as the "Land of Looms and Lores."
  • Historical Significance:
    • St. Angelo Fort: Built by the Portuguese in 1505, this historical fort offers panoramic views of the Arabian Sea and reflects the colonial history of the region.
    • Arakkal Museum: Located in the Arakkal Palace, this museum showcases the history and artifacts of the Arakkal family, the only Muslim royal family in Kerala.
    • Mappila Bay: Known for its historical significance as a trading port and its role in the region's maritime history.
  • Tourist Attractions:
    • Payyambalam Beach: A popular and serene beach known for its picturesque sunsets and relaxed atmosphere, ideal for a tranquil getaway.
    • Muzhappilangad Beach: The longest drive-in beach in Asia, where visitors can drive along the sandy shore and enjoy the scenic beauty of the Arabian Sea.
    • Vismaya Water Theme Park: A popular amusement park offering various water rides and entertainment options for families and tourists.
  • Natural Beauty:
    • Parassinikkadavu Snake Park: A unique park dedicated to the conservation and study of snakes, offering educational exhibits and a chance to see various snake species.
    • Valapattanam River: Known for its scenic beauty and the traditional boat-building industry, which adds to the charm of the river.
  • Economy:
    • Handloom Industry: Kannur is renowned for its traditional handloom products, including the famous Kannur textiles and Kasavu sarees.
    • Cashew Processing: The district has a significant cashew processing industry, contributing to its economic development.
  • Cultural Significance:
    • Theyyam: A traditional ritual dance form performed in Kannur, known for its vibrant costumes, elaborate makeup, and religious significance.
    • Festivals: The district celebrates various festivals, including the Kannur Pooram, which is known for its colorful processions and traditional performances.
  • Educational Institutions:
    • Kannur University: A prominent university offering a range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and research programs.
    • NIT Calicut: Located near Kannur, the National Institute of Technology Calicut is a leading institution in engineering education and research.
  • Transport and Connectivity:
    • Road and Rail: Kannur is well-connected by road and rail, with Kannur Railway Station serving as a major junction in the northern part of Kerala.
    • Nearest Airport: The district is accessible from Kannur International Airport, located approximately 25 kilometers away, offering domestic and international flights.
  • Conclusion: Kannur is a district that offers a rich blend of historical heritage, natural beauty, and vibrant culture, making it a compelling destination for visitors and locals alike.
