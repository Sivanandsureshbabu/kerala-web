DISTRICTS OF KERALA

Idukki

Overview:

  • Location: Idukki is a district located in the central part of Kerala, known for its mountainous terrain, lush green landscapes, and rich biodiversity. It is often referred to as the "Spice Garden of Kerala" due to its extensive spice plantations.
  • Natural Beauty:
    • Munnar: A popular hill station in Idukki, renowned for its tea plantations, misty mountains, and picturesque landscapes. It is one of the most visited tourist destinations in Kerala.
    • Thekkady: Home to the Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary, Thekkady offers a unique experience of wildlife, including elephants, tigers, and a variety of bird species. Boating on Periyar Lake is a major attraction.
    • Idukki Arch Dam: The second-largest arch dam in Asia, built across the Periyar River, offers stunning views and is a marvel of engineering.
  • Tourist Attractions:
    • Eravikulam National Park: A UNESCO World Heritage site, this park is home to the endangered Nilgiri Tahr and the famous Neelakurinji flowers that bloom once every twelve years.
    • Anamudi Peak: The highest peak in South India, located within Eravikulam National Park, it is a major trekking destination and offers breathtaking views of the Western Ghats.
    • Ramakkalmedu: A hill station known for its panoramic views of the Western Ghats and the neighboring state of Tamil Nadu, as well as its strong winds, making it a site for wind farms.
  • Economy:
    • Spice Plantations: Idukki is a major producer of spices like cardamom, pepper, and cinnamon, contributing significantly to its economy.
    • Hydroelectric Power: The district is a key player in Kerala’s power sector, with several hydroelectric projects, including the Idukki Hydroelectric Project, generating a significant portion of the state’s electricity.
  • Cultural Significance:
    • Tribal Heritage: Idukki has a significant tribal population, with tribes like the Mannan and Muthuvan contributing to the cultural diversity of the region.
    • Festivals: The district celebrates various traditional festivals, including Onam and Vishu, along with tribal festivals unique to the indigenous communities.
  • Educational Institutions:
    • College of Engineering, Munnar: A premier engineering institute located in the hill station of Munnar, offering quality education and research opportunities.
  • Transport and Connectivity:
    • Road Connectivity: Idukki is well-connected by road, with scenic routes passing through hills and forests, linking it to major cities in Kerala and Tamil Nadu.
    • Nearest Railway Stations: The nearest railway stations are Kottayam and Aluva, providing access to the district.
    • Nearest Airport: Cochin International Airport is the closest airport, located approximately 110 kilometers away.
  • Tourism and Economy: Idukki is a district that captivates with its natural beauty, rich biodiversity, and cultural heritage, making it an essential destination for nature lovers and adventurers.

Idukki is a captivating district that blends its rich biodiversity, scenic beauty, and cultural heritage, making it a must-visit destination for nature enthusiasts and adventurers.
