DISTRICTS OF KERALA

Wayanad

Overview:

  • Location: Wayanad is a district located in the northeastern part of Kerala, nestled in the Western Ghats. It is known for its lush green landscapes, wildlife sanctuaries, and rich tribal culture.
  • Natural Beauty:
    • Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary: Part of the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve, this sanctuary is home to a variety of flora and fauna, including elephants, tigers, and leopards.
    • Edakkal Caves: These ancient caves are famous for their prehistoric petroglyphs, dating back to the Neolithic age.
    • Banasura Sagar Dam: The largest earthen dam in India, offering breathtaking views of the surrounding hills and opportunities for boating and trekking.
  • Tourist Attractions:
    • Chembra Peak: The highest peak in Wayanad, popular among trekkers, with a heart-shaped lake located halfway up the trail.
    • Pookode Lake: A scenic freshwater lake surrounded by forests, offering boating facilities and a serene environment.
  • Cultural Significance:
    • Tribal Heritage: Wayanad has a significant tribal population, with various tribes like the Paniyas and Kurichiyas contributing to the region's cultural diversity.
    • Folklore Museum: The museum showcases the art, culture, and traditions of Wayanad's indigenous tribes.
  • Economy:
    • Agriculture: Wayanad's economy is primarily based on agriculture, with major crops including coffee, tea, spices, and bananas.
    • Tourism: The district's natural beauty and cultural heritage attract tourists, contributing significantly to its economy.
  • Educational Institutions:
    • Wayanad Institute of Medical Sciences: A leading medical college in the region, providing quality education and healthcare services.
  • Transport and Connectivity:
    • Road Connectivity: Wayanad is well-connected by road to major cities in Kerala and neighboring states, making it easily accessible for tourists.
    • Nearest Railway Stations: Kozhikode and Mysore are the nearest railway stations, connecting Wayanad to other parts of India.
  • Tourism and Culture: Wayanad offers a tranquil escape into nature with its rich wildlife, breathtaking landscapes, and vibrant tribal culture, making it a unique destination in Kerala.

Wayanad is a district that captivates with its natural beauty and cultural diversity, providing an unforgettable experience for visitors.
