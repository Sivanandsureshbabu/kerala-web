DISTRICTS OF KERALA

Kottayam

Overview:

  • Location: Kottayam is a district located in the southwestern part of Kerala, known for its scenic landscapes, rubber plantations, and significant contributions to education and literature. It is often referred to as the "Land of Letters, Lakes, and Latex."
  • Historical Significance:
    • CMS College: One of the oldest colleges in India, established in 1817, which played a key role in the educational and literary development of the region.
    • St. Mary's Orthodox Syrian Church: Also known as Kottayam Cheriyapally, this historic church was built in 1579 and is renowned for its ancient architecture and murals.
  • Tourist Attractions:
    • Vembanad Lake: The longest lake in India, offering houseboat cruises and serene views of the surrounding backwaters, making it a popular destination for tourists.
    • Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary: A haven for bird watchers, this sanctuary on the banks of Vembanad Lake is home to a wide variety of migratory and resident bird species.
    • Vaikom Mahadeva Temple: An ancient temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, known for its religious significance and the annual Vaikathashtami festival.
  • Natural Beauty:
    • Western Ghats: The district is part of the Western Ghats, offering lush green landscapes, hills, and forests that attract nature enthusiasts and trekkers.
    • Illikkal Kallu: A popular trekking destination, known for its unique rock formations and breathtaking views of the surrounding hills.
  • Economy:
    • Rubber Plantations: Kottayam is a major hub for rubber cultivation in Kerala, with extensive rubber plantations contributing significantly to the district's economy.
    • Publishing Industry: Kottayam is the center of the publishing industry in Kerala, housing several major publishing houses and newspapers.
  • Cultural Significance:
    • Literature: Kottayam has a rich literary tradition, being the birthplace of several renowned Malayalam writers and poets.
    • Festivals: The district is known for celebrating various religious and cultural festivals, including the Vaikathashtami and Ettumanoor Utsavam.
  • Educational Institutions:
    • Mahatma Gandhi University: A leading university in Kerala, offering a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across various disciplines.
    • CMS College: One of the oldest educational institutions in India, known for its contributions to higher education and research.
  • Transport and Connectivity:
    • Road and Rail: Kottayam is well-connected by road and rail, with Kottayam Railway Station being a major hub for trains in the region.
    • Proximity to Airports: The district is accessible from Cochin International Airport, located approximately 90 kilometers away.
  • Tourism and Economy: Kottayam is a district that beautifully blends its rich cultural heritage, educational prominence, and natural beauty, making it a significant destination in Kerala.

Kottayam is a charming district that blends natural beauty, cultural heritage, and modern amenities, making it one of Kerala's must-visit destinations.
